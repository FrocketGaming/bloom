          How Bloom inserts text
          

            
            
            
          

          

        

        
        

          Suggestions while typing
          

            
            
          

          
After the first three characters of a trigger, such as ;si, a short list of matching snippets opens at your text cursor. Up and Down move, Enter or Tab inserts, Esc closes. Apps that don’t report their cursor position just don’t show it.

        

        

          Undo with Backspace
          

            
            
          

          
Press Backspace right after Bloom expands a snippet to erase it and get the trigger you typed back. Any other key, or a click, means you have moved on, and Backspace works as usual. Snippets that place the cursor inside the text are never undone this way.

        

        

          Most used snippets first
          

            
            
          

          
Bloom keeps a small record of how often and how recently you use each snippet, in usage.toml, and lists your favourites first in suggestions and in search. The record fades over a couple of weeks, so a new favourite takes over quickly. Turning this off stops the recording and goes back to the order in your files. Delete usage.toml to start over.

        

        

          Snippets folder
          

          
Snippets are plain TOML files. Edit them in any editor and Bloom reloads them when you save.
